Software Development

CWARE develops custom business software, portals, internal tools and integrations. Engagements can cover scoping, design, implementation, deployment, handover and ongoing support.

Who it is for

Businesses whose current applications or manual processes no longer fit the way they need to work.

The problem it solves

Teams work around disconnected systems or software that cannot support the required business process.

Integrations

Web applications, APIs, databases and existing business systems. The supported interface and data requirements are confirmed during discovery.

Relevant teams and industries

Business operations across sectors, with scope based on the actual workflow and users.

CAPABILITIES

What the work includes.

01

Custom builds, end to end

Scope, design, build and deploy web applications, portals, integrations and internal tools. Handover includes the source code and the work delivered.

02

Developers in your team

Add experienced people to your backlog and delivery standards. Scale the team around the work without creating permanent roles for a short-term requirement.

03

Support after go-live

Keep systems running with hosting, monitoring, fixes and improvements under a support arrangement scoped to your needs.

04

Room to change

Add business rules, reports, workflow steps and integrations as your needs develop. The software can evolve around your process.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Answers before you enquire.

Who owns the delivered source code?

CWARE’s published development approach includes ownership of the delivered work and source code, with documented handover. The agreement records the detailed terms.

Do we need a full specification before speaking to CWARE?

No. Start with the problem, the current process and the intended outcome. CWARE helps turn that into a scope you can review.

What happens after go-live?

Hosting, monitoring, fixes and improvements can be provided under an agreed support arrangement.
