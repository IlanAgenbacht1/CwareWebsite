Contract, permanent & outsourced teams

IT Recruitment

CWARE sources and screens IT professionals for contract appointments, permanent placements and managed teams. Technical screening is informed by CWARE’s own software-development work.

Discuss a role ↗Request pricingHow it works ↓

Who it is for

Companies hiring developers, testers, analysts, support staff and IT operations professionals.

The problem it solves

The team needs relevant technical skills without spending interview time on poorly matched candidates.

Integrations

The recruitment desk uses CWARE Recruit for candidate records, pipeline status and client-ready submissions.

Relevant teams and industries

Employers with IT and software-delivery roles, across South Africa.

CAPABILITIES

What the work includes.

01

Contract appointments

Bring people into projects, migrations and delivery pushes for the agreed duration, billed monthly and administered by CWARE.

02

Permanent placements

Work through sourcing, screening and shortlisting for a role in your own team. Spend interview time on candidates selected against the brief.

03

Managed delivery teams

When the requirement is output, engage a team working under CWARE to your priorities, with accountability for delivery.

04

Technical screening

Candidates are screened by people who build software and understand the work the role requires.

IN PRACTICE

Our recruitment desk runs on CWARE Recruit.

Candidate records, structured CVs, live pipeline status and client-ready submissions are managed in our own recruitment platform. Ask for the position of your search at any stage.

HOW IT WORKS

The working sequence.

  1. 01

    Brief the role

    Agree the work, requirements and what success looks like.

  2. 02

    Search and screen

    Source candidates and conduct technical screening.

  3. 03

    Meet the shortlist

    Review selected candidates and coordinate interviews.

  4. 04

    Place and follow up

    Confirm the appointment and check in after the start.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Answers before you enquire.

Do you place permanent and contract staff?

Yes. CWARE supports permanent appointments, contract roles and managed delivery teams.

Can you help with remote roles?

CWARE works across South Africa with on-site, hybrid and remote placements, subject to the role and candidate availability.

How are candidates screened?

The process starts with a role brief and includes sourcing, screening and technical assessment before a shortlist is presented.
