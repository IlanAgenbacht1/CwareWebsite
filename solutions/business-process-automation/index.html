Business Process Automation

Business process automation connects agreed steps, data and approvals inside or between applications. CWARE scopes the current process and builds the required rules and system connections.

Start with a repeated hand-off

Examples include approved time flowing into invoicing, event payments updating a ledger, or a customer conversation creating a record.

Use an existing product where it fits

HR Portal connects leave, timesheets and invoicing. Events Portal connects member entries and payment records. Custom development can address requirements outside those products.

Keep exceptions visible

Agree what happens when a record is incomplete, a payment notification repeats or a connected service fails. Include the responsible person and the required audit information in the scope.
