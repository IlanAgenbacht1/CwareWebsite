Recruitment Agencies

CWARE Recruit is designed for agencies managing candidate records, vacancies, branded submissions and business-development leads. CWARE also uses it for its own recruitment desk.

The work it supports

Capture and search candidates, prepare anonymised Word CVs, track vacancies and manage reference-derived leads.

The integration starting point

Bring an example CV, your current candidate export and one client brief. Review the quality of imported records and the submission format before rollout.
