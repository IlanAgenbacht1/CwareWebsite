Question

How much does CWARE Connect cost?

CWARE Connect starts from R2 500 excl. VAT per month per WhatsApp number. The final quote depends on functionality and integrations. Setup and messaging charges must be confirmed as part of the quote.

What the starting figure means

It is the published subscription starting point per number. It is not a complete project quote or a message-volume tariff.

What affects the scope

The number of WhatsApp numbers, customer journeys, target systems and team workflow help define the implementation.

How to compare costs

Compare the subscription, implementation work, messaging costs and support scope on the same basis. The calculator shows only the published subscription baseline.
