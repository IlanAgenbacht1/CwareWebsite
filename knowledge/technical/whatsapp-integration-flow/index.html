How CWARE Connect Fits into Your Systems

Customers use WhatsApp. Meta’s official WhatsApp Business Platform provides the messaging channel. CWARE Connect applies automation and routing, connects to the agreed business systems and gives the team a web application for handling the work.

Messaging channel

The official WhatsApp Business Platform carries the business messaging interaction.

Application workflow

Connect handles contacts, broadcasts, automation, reporting and routing to a person when needed.

System connection

The integration reads or writes the agreed data through the target system’s available interface. Permissions, validation and failure handling are scoped for that connection.

Implementation example

A customer provides details in a guided conversation. The integration passes the reviewed data to the business system, and the team handles any exception in the agreed workflow. This reference describes component roles, not a public API contract.
