Applicant tracking & agency CRM

CWARE Recruit

CWARE Recruit is a browser-based applicant tracking and agency CRM platform for South African recruitment agencies. It combines AI-assisted CV capture, candidate search, branded submissions and business-development leads.

Get a CWARE Recruit demo ↗Request pricingHow it works ↓

Who it is for

Recruitment agencies that manage candidate delivery and business development.

The problem it solves

Candidate details, CV formatting and sales follow-up are spread across documents, inboxes and separate lists.

Integrations

PDF and Word CV capture, verified CSV import and formatted Word CV export. Connections to external systems are assessed against your requirements.

Relevant teams and industries

Recruitment agencies and internal recruitment operations.

CAPABILITIES

What the work includes.

01

AI-assisted CV capture

Upload a PDF or Word CV to capture education, work history, skills, salary and notice period. Your recruiter reviews and corrects the record on screen. Verified bulk CSV import helps bring an existing database across.

02

Search the candidate pool

Find candidates by multiple skills, location and relocation preferences. Standardised job titles help bring different descriptions of the same role into one search.

03

Client-ready CVs

Generate formatted Word CVs using internal, external or white-label templates. Branded, anonymised submissions help protect candidate details during the introduction.

04

One candidate record

Keep experience, education, interviews, references and documents together. Edit the details in place, with pipeline stage and status alongside the profile.

IN PRACTICE

Your next client may already be in your database.

Reference mining turns relevant candidate references into business-development leads, individually or in bulk. Score leads, assign owners and record activity from first contact to a won opportunity, then convert that lead into a client record.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Answers before you enquire.

Can Recruit read PDF and Word CVs?

Yes. Recruit captures details from PDF and Word CVs. A recruiter reviews and corrects the structured record before using it.

Can we import our existing candidate database?

Verified bulk CSV import is available. We review the source fields and data quality when planning the import.

Can client submissions be anonymised?

Yes. Recruit supports formatted Word CVs with internal, external and white-label templates, including branded and anonymised submissions.
