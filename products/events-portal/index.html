Events, members & payments

Events Portal

CWARE Events Portal manages member registration, event entries, payments and reconciliation in one system. Families enter and pay through a member portal while administrators manage eligibility, fees and the ledger.

Request a 30-minute walkthrough ↗Request pricingHow it works ↓

Who it is for

Membership organisations and event organisers managing recurring entries, family accounts and payments.

The problem it solves

Entries and payments have to be matched across spreadsheets, messages and bank records.

Integrations

PayFast card and Instant EFT payments, manual EFT proof verification, email receipts and event exports.

Relevant teams and industries

Swimming academies, clubs and organisations running member-based events. Other event formats are assessed during scoping.

CAPABILITIES

What the work includes.

01

One account for the family

An adult account holds the members they are responsible for and their documents. Families see eligible events, choose participants and extras, sign agreements and track payment status.

02

Run the season

Configure registration phases, late surcharges, sessions and entry costs. Maintain your fee schedule, control eligibility through groups and export participants, officials and documents.

03

A ledger you can reconcile

Record each charge, payment, refund and credit as its own line. The balance comes from those transactions, with manual EFT proof upload and administrator verification alongside PayFast payments.

04

Keep historical entries intact

Each entry retains the age, category, club and fee recorded at submission. Changes to a member’s current details do not rewrite last season’s entry records.

IN PRACTICE

Already running a competitive swimming season.

The portal supports family registrations, entries, officials rosters, extras, document generation and reconciliation at an established competitive swimming academy. Terminology and configuration can be adapted for other organisations. A reference call is available on request.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Answers before you enquire.

Can one parent manage more than one member?

Yes. An adult account can hold the members they are responsible for and manage their event entries.

How are refunds and credits recorded?

Charges, payments, refunds and credits are separate ledger entries. Refunds can be recorded as account credit or cash with an audited reason.

Do old event records change when a member changes category?

No. Each entry retains the age, category, club and fee recorded at submission.
