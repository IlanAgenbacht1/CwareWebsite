People, time, leave & invoicing

HR Portal

CWARE HR Portal connects employee records, timesheets, leave and invoicing. It supports manager approvals and payroll data exports, with access controlled through Employee, Manager, HR and Admin roles.

Get a HR Portal demo ↗Request pricingHow it works ↓

Who it is for

Growing companies that need connected employee administration, approved time and client invoicing.

The problem it solves

The same employee and time information is repeatedly captured in leave records, payroll preparation and invoices.

Integrations

CSV reporting for payroll and branded invoice PDF export. Approved leave posts into timesheets inside the portal. External payroll integration is scoped separately.

Relevant teams and industries

Professional services, software teams and other companies managing project time and employee administration.

CAPABILITIES

What the work includes.

01

One file per employee

Keep employment details, banking, addresses, emergency contacts and documents together. Staff can maintain permitted details, while HR receives change notifications with bank numbers masked.

02

Time captured against real work

Capture time by client, project and task, then submit it weekly for manager approval. Employees can only book time against projects assigned to them.

03

Connected leave and timesheets

Manage live balances and accrual policies by leave type. Working-day calculations account for weekends and South African public holidays. Approved leave posts into the timesheet.

04

Invoicing without repeated capture

Create invoices with line items, discounts, dual tax rates and sequential numbering. Export branded PDFs, clone invoices and use time reports by client, project or employee, with CSV exports for payroll.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Answers before you enquire.

Is HR Portal a payroll calculation engine?

The documented scope includes employee and payroll data, approved time, CSV reporting and invoicing. Statutory payroll calculations are not claimed here; discuss your payroll system and required hand-off with CWARE.

Does approved leave appear in timesheets?

Yes. Approved leave posts into the timesheet. Leave calculations account for weekends and South African public holidays.

Can the portal run on our own servers?

Yes. CWARE can host and maintain it, or it can run on your own servers.
