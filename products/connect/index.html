WhatsApp Business Platform

CWARE Connect

CWARE Connect is a managed WhatsApp Business Platform solution. It connects customer conversations to automation, a shared web application and business systems through Meta’s official WhatsApp Business Platform.

Get a CWARE Connect demo ↗Request pricingHow it works ↓

Who it is for

Businesses that need to manage customer onboarding, enquiries, notifications or follow-ups on WhatsApp.

The problem it solves

Staff repeat the same replies and manually transfer information from conversations into business systems.

Integrations

CRM, ERP and database integrations are scoped against the available APIs, permissions and required workflows. Support for a particular system is confirmed during discovery.

Relevant teams and industries

Customer-facing businesses with repeatable sales, onboarding or service processes.

CAPABILITIES

What the work includes.

01

Capture and onboard in chat

Guide customers through questions and capture the answers directly in your CRM or database. Turn an enquiry into a qualified lead or an onboarded customer without retyping the conversation.

02

Automate routine support

Handle recurring questions about hours, pricing, order status and statements. Route the conversation to a person when it needs individual attention.

03

Broadcast and notify

Send targeted campaigns, order updates and account notifications. Support loyalty registrations and follow-ups through the channel your customers already use.

04

Build customer journeys

Set up multi-step onboarding, payment reminders and post-service feedback. Agreed rules drive each step so your team does not have to remember every follow-up.

IN PRACTICE

Official infrastructure. A fully managed business platform.

CWARE builds and runs the layer around the official Meta WhatsApp Business Platform: contacts, broadcasts, automation, reporting and connections to your existing records. Customers use WhatsApp while your team manages the work in one place.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Answers before you enquire.

Can CWARE Connect integrate WhatsApp with our CRM or ERP?

Yes, subject to the system’s available APIs and access permissions. CWARE scopes the data, actions and rules before building the connection.

Can we automate customer onboarding?

Yes. Guided questions capture information in the conversation and pass it into the connected business system.

How much does CWARE Connect cost?

The published starting price is R2 500 excl. VAT per month per WhatsApp number. Confirm setup, messaging charges, functionality and integrations in your quote.

Can a person take over an automated conversation?

Yes. Connect supports routing to a person when a conversation needs individual attention.
